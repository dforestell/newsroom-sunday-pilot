No. 2 Ohio State had the ball for 39 minutes and 28 seconds at USC. It lost 30–18.

That's the whole story, and it's a dumb one. The Buckeyes outgained USC 464 to 338. Bo Jackson ran for 121 yards. Jeremiah Smith caught 10 balls. Luke Fahey threw for 260. Every box score number points one way.

The scoreboard points the other. USC went up 14–3 in the first quarter and Ohio State didn't score a single point in the third. Jayden Maiava only needed 22 throws, going 15-of-22 for 222, and Trent Mosley turned five catches into 101 yards and a touchdown.

Fahey went 21-of-38. When you control the ball for two-thirds of the game and finish with 18 points, the yards are just exercise.

So USC is 7–2, Ohio State is 6–2, and somewhere a Buckeyes fan is sending me the time-of-possession chart like it's a receipt. It's not. It's a long drive to a field goal.
