Brandon Aiyuk filed his reinstatement paperwork Wednesday. Drew Rosenhaus says he's healthy and will show up at the 49ers facility Friday afternoon.

His last NFL snap was Oct. 20, 2024, in Kansas City. That's the day he tore his ACL, MCL and meniscus. Since then the Niners voided about $27 million of his guarantees, moved him to reserve/left squad, then to reserve/did not report.

In September, Kyle Shanahan got asked about a reunion with a receiver room full of injuries. His answer was one word. "No."

So Aiyuk is reporting to a building that already told him he's done there. Lynch and Shanahan have said it more than once. The likely play, per ESPN, is a release if he's reinstated.

Here's my call. Somebody takes a flier on a 28-year-old who put up 1,342 yards and second-team All-Pro in 2023, and it isn't San Francisco. Trade deadline is Tuesday at 4 ET. The Commanders already signed Stefon Diggs, so his Jayden Daniels dream might need a new zip code.

Friday afternoon. Bring a visitor badge.
