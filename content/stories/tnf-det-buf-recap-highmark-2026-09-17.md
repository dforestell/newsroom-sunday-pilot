Buffalo opened the new house and collected rent. Josh Allen finished with five total touchdowns. Detroit climbed from 0–21 to a 41–31 deficit that still felt like unpaid rent.

I picked Detroit to win in Buffalo’s new house. Here’s the part I got wrong, and it isn’t “the Lions are bad.” Detroit scored 31 and Goff threw four touchdowns. What I got wrong was the first twenty minutes. Buffalo scored on its first three drives, and [by the 11:48 mark of the second quarter it was 21–0](https://www.espn.com/nfl/recap/_/gameId/401872932). After that, Detroit never got closer than ten. Every time the Lions scored in the second half, Buffalo answered with a touchdown: a fifteen-play, seventy-five-yard drive after St. Brown’s, and James Cook after LaPorta’s. My comeback theory needed stops. Detroit got exactly one in the second half, a punt with 8:32 left, and went three-and-out right after it.

## The ceremony sold first

Chyron Carl’s read: the broadcast sold Allen’s first Highmark intro before the game had a score. Platform put the house on the marquee; football filled in later.

## The referendum passed. The night didn’t.

Down 27–10 early in the third, [Detroit went for it on fourth-and-5 from the Buffalo 40](https://www.espn.com/nfl/playbyplay/_/gameId/401872932). Goff hit Sam LaPorta for eighteen, and four plays later Amon-Ra St. Brown scored. Wes calls it the right decision on the right down. The final still belonged to Buffalo.

## Allen night, stamped

Three passing touchdowns, two rushing, and the first regular-season night in the new building. Postcard Pete stamps it. He says it was a great night and he won’t argue with anyone who says so. The season verdict waits.

## Cook closes it

James Cook ran 21 times for 135 yards, and his one-yard touchdown with 2:50 left made it 41–24. Detroit’s late score was the kind you get when the other team already has the keys.

## Lions defense, second-half edition

The offense did its share: 21 second-half points. [Buffalo had three real possessions after halftime](https://www.espn.com/nfl/playbyplay/_/gameId/401872932): touchdown, punt, touchdown. The fourth was Allen running out the clock. A comeback needs the other side of the ball to say “no” more than once. Detroit’s defense said it one time, and the offense went three-and-out right after.

New house. Same Bills. My Lions pick paid the first month’s rent.
