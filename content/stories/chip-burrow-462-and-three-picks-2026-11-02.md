Joe Burrow threw for 462 yards and five touchdowns on Sunday. He also threw three interceptions, and the Bengals needed overtime to beat a Titans team that is now 1–7.

That's the Bengals in one box score. Burrow went 38-of-48. Tee Higgins caught 10 for 155 and two scores. Ja'Marr Chase caught 11 for 141. Cincinnati put up 510 yards and trailed 13–0 after one quarter anyway.

Tennessee hung around all day. Cam Ward threw for 304 with no picks, Tony Pollard ran for 117 and two touchdowns, and the Titans led 36–29 with under four minutes left after Ward hit Wan'Dale Robinson and then found Gunnar Helm for two.

Burrow tied it with 1:17 left on an 18-yard throw to Higgins, then won it himself on a 9-yard run in overtime.

Great finish. But when your quarterback has a 462-yard day, a 1–7 team should not be the one holding the lead in the fourth quarter. The Bengals are 5–2 and still make every week feel like a hostage video.
