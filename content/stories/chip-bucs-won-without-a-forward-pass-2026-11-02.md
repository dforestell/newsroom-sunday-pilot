Tampa Bay beat Atlanta 16–0 on Sunday and did not complete a single pass.

Baker Mayfield went 0-for-3. The Bucs finished with minus-2 passing yards. That's not a typo. That's a team that looked at the forward pass and decided it was optional.

Instead they ran it 56 times for 324 yards. Bucky Irving had 170 and the only touchdown. Kenny Gainwell added 140. Chase McLaughlin kicked three field goals, and that was plenty, because Atlanta never got close.

Michael Penix Jr. went 8-of-24 for 125 yards and four interceptions. His passer rating was 12.0. The Falcons went 0-for-12 on third down. Drake London saw six targets and caught one for eight yards.

So the scoreboard says one team had a quarterback who threw four picks and the other had a quarterback who never completed a ball, and the second one won by 16. Nobody on either sideline should be allowed near a whiteboard this week.

Both teams are 3–5. Somehow that feels generous to one of them.
