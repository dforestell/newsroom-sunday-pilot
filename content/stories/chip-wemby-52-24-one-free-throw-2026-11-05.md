Spurs 112, Mavericks 111. Victor Wembanyama went for 52 points, 24 rebounds and eight blocks. Both the points and the boards are career highs.

Per the AP, he's the first player in 50 years with 50, 20 and five blocks in one game. He did it with a virus, and he rolled his ankle in the fourth.

He had 29, 15 and five blocks by halftime. San Antonio led by 19 early in the third. Then the Spurs went cold from deep, finishing 5-of-36 from three, and P.J. Washington put Dallas up 103-100 with 5:38 left.

So the most ridiculous stat line of the season came down to the free-throw line. Wemby missed his first three in the last 2:10, then made one with 24 seconds left. That was the game.

A week ago I canceled the sample-size funeral. Now the crowd's chanting MVP. San Antonio has won six straight and sits at 6-2.

My call: he's the MVP favorite by Thanksgiving. And somebody on that roster has to make a three so he doesn't have to drop 52 every night.
