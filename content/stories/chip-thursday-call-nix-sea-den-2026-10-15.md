Four out of ten throws end up somewhere other than a Broncos receiver's hands, and I'm putting my good name on one of them landing in a Seahawks jersey.

Now the part where you get to yell at me. Nix has three interceptions in 162 attempts. That's one pick every fifty-odd throws, and Denver won't throw fifty times on a Thursday. If you're a Broncos fan, that's your whole case, and it's a good one.

So understand what I'm claiming and what I'm not. I'm not saying Nix is bad, and I'm not saying Denver loses. I'm saying his misses finally run into the defense that's best at catching them. A ball in the dirt is harmless against most teams. Against this one it's a coin flip with feathers on it.
