My Friday call had two halves: Luka Dončić scores 35 or more, and the Lakers win at Golden State. Luka handled his half. He put up 37 in 37 minutes.

The Lakers handled nothing. Warriors 112, Lakers 95.

That one stings, because I was right about the hard part. Luka went 12-of-27, hit six threes, and went 7-of-8 at the line. If you only bet the star, you got paid. I didn't. I said both, so it's a miss, and it goes on the record with the rest of them on Monday.

Here's what makes it worse. The Lakers were up 38–15 after the first quarter, and the lead got to 25. After one quarter I was already drafting the victory lap.

Then Golden State won the next three quarters 97–57.

The Warriors made 22 threes on 46 tries. Steph Curry had 31 with eight of them, Brandin Podziemski added 21, and Georges Niang came off the bench and went 3-for-3 from deep like he'd been saving it. Meanwhile the Lakers shot 11-of-39 from three, turned it over 22 times, and picked up four technicals. Austin Reaves scored 21 and turned it over eight times. Collin Sexton went 0-for-6.

So no, I'm not blaming Luka. A guy who gives you 37 and a +9 on a 17-point loss did his job. The miss is mine for trusting the other four guys on the floor with a 25-point cushion.

Lesson filed: when the star is the whole case, just call the star.
